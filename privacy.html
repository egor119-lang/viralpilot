Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ViralPilot may process information when a creator connects an account, prepares content or uses supported publishing features.

1. Information we may process

  • basic connected-account information returned by an authorized platform;
  • authorization tokens required to access platform APIs on your behalf;
  • content provided to the application, including images, video, audio, captions and scheduling data;
  • technical status information related to rendering, scheduling and publishing operations.

2. How information is used

  • connecting an authorized creator account;
  • displaying account and publishing capabilities;
  • preparing and organizing content;
  • submitting user-approved content through supported APIs;
  • maintaining application security and troubleshooting.

3. Authorization tokens

Access and refresh tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed publicly. ViralPilot uses such tokens only for authorized API requests.

4. Sharing

ViralPilot does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a connected third-party platform when necessary to perform an action requested and authorized by the user.

5. Retention

Data should be retained only as long as needed to provide requested functionality. Users may disconnect their account and remove locally stored content and credentials.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and application data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Third-party platforms

Connected third-party platforms may independently process information under their own policies and terms.

8. User choices

Users may decline permissions, disconnect an account, revoke platform access through platform settings, and remove locally stored application data.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project operator through TikTok: @povstories.world.
